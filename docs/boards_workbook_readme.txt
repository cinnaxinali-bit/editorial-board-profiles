Editorial boards of SMJ, Organization Science, ASQ, AMJ and Management Science: member profiles (November 2026)
What is in this workbook:
• People: one row per person (each person once, however many boards they sit on), with their profile and a column per journal showing the role(s) they hold there. Sorted by highest role (editors first), then surname.
• Board roles: one row per seat, as listed on each journal's editorial-board page, joined to the person's profile. Filter by Journal, Role or Role group to see one journal's editors, associate editors or review board.
• Summary: live counts (formulas): seats by journal and role, journal by area, editors and associate editors by area, people by area and position, and how many of these boards people sit on.
Rosters and coverage:
• SMJ: co-editors, associate editors, guest associate editors, consulting/founding/collections/media editors and the Editorial Review Board (Wiley page, November 2026).
• Organization Science: editor-in-chief, deputy editors, senior editors, outreach editors and the Editorial Review Board (INFORMS page). Its review board is the 358-person list profiled first.
• ASQ: editor, deputy editor, associate editors, book review, communications and social media editors, Editorial Board and Methods Advisory Panel (SAGE page). Managing-editor staff are left out.
• AMJ: editor, deputy editors, associate editors and Editorial Review Board (Academy of Management page, November 2026).
• Management Science: the Editor-in-Chief plus the department editors and associate editors of the Strategy, Organizations and Sustainability departments only (INFORMS page updated 13 November 2026).
• Role groups: Editor = editor-in-chief, editor, co-editor, deputy editor or department editor; Associate / Senior Editor = associate, senior or guest associate editor; Review board = editorial (review) board; Other editorial role = consulting, outreach, book review, media, methods panel and similar roles.
Columns:
• Website (faculty page) = official profile at the current institution; Personal website and CV link are filled when one was found. Links come from pages and search results, never constructed.
• Institution (current) can differ from "Affiliation(s) on board pages": board pages are often out of date. "Moved since listing?" flags these.
• Position = normalized rank (Assistant / Associate / Full / Other). Endowed, chaired and UK/European "Professor" titles count as Full; UK Senior Lecturer and Reader count as Associate; emeritus, teaching-track and research-staff roles are Other. "Exact title" keeps the official wording.
• Strategy & OT vs OB: OB = micro, psychological research on individuals and teams; Strategy & OT = macro research on strategy, innovation, entrepreneurship, governance, organization theory and economic sociology (including qualitative studies of work); Other = a primary discipline outside management (e.g., economists and finance scholars in Management Science's Sustainability department). "Borderline call?" marks hybrids; "Classification rationale" explains each call.
• Subfield is a standardized category; "Subfield (detail)" keeps the more specific label.
Matching people across journals:
• Names were matched across pages after normalizing accents, initials, particles (Di Stefano / Distefano) and common nicknames (Drew / Andrew, Raj / Prithwiraj). Cases needing judgment are listed in data/people/name_overrides.csv in the project repository; for example, Elizabeth Lauren Campbell (UC San Diego) and Elizabeth Margaret Campbell (Minnesota) are different people.
Caveats:
• Titles change every July with promotions and moves; check a profile before relying on it.
• Confidence = high / medium / low summarizes how well sources agreed; low-confidence and not-yet-researched rows are shaded yellow.
• Research-topic summaries are condensed from each person's own profile, CV and recent papers.
